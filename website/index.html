    A 12-part lecture series: how neuromodulation works, the main therapies from spinal cord, DRG and peripheral nerve stimulation to intrathecal pumps and brain stimulation, choosing patients, managing complications, measuring outcomes, and where the field is heading.

    A lecture series on peptide and metabolic therapies: the hormone axes they act on, incretin drugs for weight loss, repair peptides such as BPC-157 and TB-500, NAD+ and mitochondrial compounds, and what the evidence does and doesn’t support.
