Video

Video series and short explainers, grouped by project. Each collection has its own page.

  • Spine biomechanics

    A 40-part lecture series in 20 sessions, from the basic mechanics of bone, disc and ligament through alignment, degeneration, trauma and fragility fracture, to how decompression, fusion, motion-preserving implants and interventional procedures change the way the spine carries load.

    40 videos

  • Neuromodulation

    A 12-part lecture series: how neuromodulation works, the main therapies from spinal cord, DRG and peripheral nerve stimulation to intrathecal pumps and brain stimulation, choosing patients, managing complications, measuring outcomes, and where the field is heading.

    12 videos

  • Peptides and metabolic therapies

    A lecture series on peptide and metabolic therapies: the hormone axes they act on, incretin drugs for weight loss, repair peptides such as BPC-157 and TB-500, NAD+ and mitochondrial compounds, and what the evidence does and doesn’t support.

    14 videos

  • Regenerative medicine, explained

    A 24-part series on what regenerative treatments are, how the main options differ, where they are used in the body, and what the research does and doesn’t show yet.

    24 videos

  • Knee pain: the treatment options

    An overview of the non-surgical options for knee pain, from injections to nerve treatments and embolization, followed by a short video on each one.

    8 videos

  • Patient stories (dramatized)

    Dramatized stories showing what living with spine, joint and bone problems can be like, and how different treatments work.

    10 videos
