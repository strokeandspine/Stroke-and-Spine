If you think you have a medical emergency, call 911. We can't give medical advice by email. If you're having thoughts of harming yourself, call or text 988.

Common questions

Where is my information stored? Only on your iPhone. There's no account, and we can't see or recover your data. To keep a copy, use More → Privacy, reminders and your data → Export all my data. iPhone backups also include the app's data.

I got a new iPhone. How do I move my data? Restore your new iPhone from an iCloud or computer backup of your old one. The app's data comes with it.

How do I delete everything? More → Privacy, reminders and your data → Delete all my data. Deleting the app also removes its data from your iPhone.

How do I start, cancel or change my Premium subscription? Start it from More → Subscription. To cancel or switch between monthly and annual, go to iPhone Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → Stroke & Spine. Cancel at least 24 hours before your renewal date to avoid being charged.

I subscribed but Premium isn't unlocked. Go to More → Subscription → Restore purchases, making sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID you used to subscribe.

How do I get a refund? Refunds are handled by Apple at https://reportaproblem.apple.com.

Steps or sleep aren't showing. Check iPhone Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → Stroke & Spine, and turn on Steps and Sleep. Sleep only appears if a device or app records it to Apple Health.

Can my doctor see my information? Only if you share it. Use More → Appointment report to create a PDF you can email, AirDrop or print.
