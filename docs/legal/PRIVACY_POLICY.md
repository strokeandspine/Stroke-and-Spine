- **You're in control.** You can export or permanently delete everything in the App at any time.

## 3. Information the App stores on your device

When you use the App, the information you enter is saved **only on your iPhone**, in the App's private storage, which iOS protects with its standard data protection:

| Information | Examples | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Profile | Optional first name, when your pain started, goals | To personalize the App |
| Health conditions you choose to enter | Kidney disease, pregnancy, blood thinners | To show the right over-the-counter medicine safety warnings |
| Daily check-ins | Pain scores, sleep quality, mood, triggers, notes | To show your trends |
| Questionnaire answers and scores | Back function, mood, anxiety and sleep questionnaires | To track changes over time |
| Warning-sign checks | Which symptoms you reported | To tell you how urgently to get care |
| Medicines you log | Product, amount, time, pain before | To track totals against label limits |
| Spine history | Surgeries, injections, scans, providers, implants, photos you attach | To keep your record in one place |
| Exercise sessions | Exercises completed, pain before and after | To guide your program |

**Apple Health.** If you allow it, the App **reads** your step count and sleep data from Apple Health to show them alongside your pain. The App does not write to Apple Health. Apple Health data is used only inside the App, never for advertising, and never shared. You can turn off access at any time in the iOS Settings app under Health → Data Access & Devices.

**Photos.** If you attach photos (for example of a discharge summary), the App only receives the specific photos you pick. They are stored inside the App.

**Reminders.** Daily reminders are scheduled on your iPhone by iOS. No information is sent to us.

## 4. Information we receive

Because your health information never leaves your device through the App, **we do not collect, receive or store it.** The only information that may reach us is:

- **Purchase information from Apple.** If you subscribe to Premium, Apple processes the payment. We don't receive your name, email address or payment details. Apple may give us anonymous, aggregated sales and subscription reports. Apple's privacy policy applies to purchases: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/
- **Crash reports and app analytics from Apple, only if you agree.** If you have chosen in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements to share data with app developers, Apple may share anonymous crash reports and usage statistics with us. These don't include the health information you enter in the App. You can change this setting at any time.
- **Messages you send us.** If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to include. We use it only to reply, and we delete it when it is no longer needed.

## 5. Sharing

We do not sell, rent, share or disclose your information to anyone, including advertisers, data brokers, insurers or employers.

Information only leaves the App when **you** choose to send it, for example when you share your appointment report PDF or data export by AirDrop, email or Messages. Once you send it, the recipient's own privacy practices apply.

## 6. Backups

iOS may include the App's data in your **iCloud or computer backups** if you have backups turned on. Apple encrypts and stores those backups. Apple's privacy policy applies to them, and we cannot access them.

## 7. Keeping your information safe

- Data is stored in the App's private storage, which iOS encrypts when your iPhone is locked with a passcode.
- You can turn on **Face ID / passcode lock** for the App in More → Privacy, reminders and your data.
- Exported files are created with iOS's strongest file protection.

Anyone who can unlock your iPhone may be able to open the App unless you turn on the App lock.

## 8. How long information is kept

Your information stays on your iPhone until you delete it. You can delete individual entries, or delete everything in **More → Privacy, reminders and your data → Delete all my data**. Deleting the App also deletes its data from your iPhone, though copies may remain in your device backups.

## 9. Your choices and rights

You can, at any time and without contacting us:

- **See** everything the App stores by browsing the App.
- **Export** all of it in More → Privacy, reminders and your data → Export all my data.
- **Delete** all of it, as described above.
- **Withdraw consent** to Apple Health access in iOS Settings.

If you live in a US state with consumer health data or consumer privacy laws (including Washington, Nevada, Connecticut, California, Colorado, Virginia, Texas and others), you have rights to access, delete and withdraw consent to the processing of your health data, and to appeal our decisions. Because we don't hold your App data on our systems, you exercise these rights directly in the App. If you have a question or want to make a request or appeal, email [PRIVACY CONTACT EMAIL] with "Privacy request" in the subject. We'll respond within 45 days. If we deny an appeal, you can contact your state Attorney General.

We do not discriminate against you for exercising any of these rights.
