### Chocolate Peanut Butter Power
*A peanut butter cup you can drink, with 50 g of protein.* — Rich chocolate, roasted peanut, creamy

**407 kcal** · 52 g protein · 37 g carbs (30 g net) · 7 g fat · 7 g fiber · 16 g sugars (0 g added)

Goals: Strength Gain, Post-Workout

- Unsweetened almond milk: 1 cup (240 g)
- Nonfat plain Greek yogurt: ⅔ cup (150 g)
- Whey protein isolate, chocolate *(supplement)*: 1 scoop (31 g)
- Powdered peanut butter: 2½ tbsp (16 g)
- Banana: ½ large, frozen (80 g)
- Unsweetened cocoa powder: 1 tbsp (5 g)
- Creatine monohydrate *(supplement)*: 1 tsp (5 g) (5 g)
- Ice: ½ cup (60 g)

1. Add the liquids to the blender first, then the soft ingredients, powders and frozen ingredients last.
2. Blend on high for 45–60 seconds until completely smooth.
3. Taste, adjust thickness with a splash of liquid or a few ice cubes, and serve.

**Why it works:** Powdered peanut butter keeps the peanut flavor with about 85% less fat than regular peanut butter, so protein makes up nearly half the calories. Greek yogurt adds slow-digesting casein and the frozen banana supplies carbs to refill energy after training.

**Swaps:** Use 1 tbsp natural peanut butter instead of the powder (+60 kcal, +7 g fat). Swap Greek yogurt for silken tofu and use plant protein to make it dairy-free.
